# EduQuest: Project Presentation & User Guide

## 1. What is EduQuest?
EduQuest is a smart educational platform that connects teachers and students. It uses **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** to make teaching easier and learning more interactive.

*   **For Teachers:** Instantly generate quizzes from notes using AI.
*   **For Students:** Take quizzes, look at results, and discuss doubts in a forum.

---

## 2. The Teacher's Journey
*How a teacher uses the App*

### Step 1: Login & Dashboard
*   The teacher logs in with their secure credentials.
*   They land on the **Teacher Dashboard**, which is their "Control Center".

### Step 2: Creating a Magic Quiz with AI
*   Instead of writing 10 questions manually, the teacher simply **pastes their lecture notes** or a chapter summary into a text box.
*   They select a subject (e.g., Physics) and difficulty (Medium).
*   **Click "Generate":** The AI reads the text and automatically creates questions with correct answers.
*   The teacher reviews them and clicks **"Publish"**.

### Step 3: Monitoring Students
*   Teachers can see a list of their enrolled students.
*   They can check **Performance Stats** to see who is passing or failing.

---

## 3. The Student's Journey
*How a student learns*

### Step 1: Seeing What's Due
*   When a student logs in, they see a personalized **Student Dashboard**.
*   It shows them a list of quizzes created by their teachers.

### Step 2: Taking a Quiz
*   The student clicks "Start Quiz".
*   They answer questions one by one.
*   **Instant Feedback:** As soon as they finish, the system grades them instantly (e.g., 85% - Grade A). No waiting for days!

---

## 4. The Collaborative Forum
*Solving doubts together*

*   **Ask:** If a student is stuck, they can post a question in the **Forum**.
*   **Answer:** Other students or the teacher can reply with answers.
*   **Vote:** Good answers get "Upvoted" so everyone knows which answer is the best.
*   **Teacher Verified:** If a teacher answers, it gets a special badges so students know it's a trusted answer.
